Your go-to place for any math question — type it below, or explore a tool

New here? Here's the idea: type any question in the box below and this app figures out what to do with it — arithmetic, algebra, graphs, all of it. Or explore a tab yourself: Graph draws pictures of equations, Equations and Calculus solve and analyze them, Matrix, Stats, and Complex cover those topics, Base-N is binary/hex, Units converts things, and Date does calendar math. Every tool shows an example so you can just try it. Nothing you type ever leaves your device.

Try:

Tap the keys like a normal calculator. The purple buttons (sin, log, π...) are for more advanced math — try 2×π or sin(30).

Type a function of x and watch it get drawn instantly — try x^2 or sin(x). Add more functions to compare them, move your mouse over the graph to read values, or hit "Find roots" to see where the line crosses zero.

Move over the graph to read values.

Linear: ax + b = 0

The simplest equations — a straight line crossing zero. 2x - 4 = 0 means a=2, b=-4.

Quadratic: ax² + bx + c = 0

For equations with an x² in them. x² - 3x + 2 = 0 means a=1, b=-3, c=2 — works even when the answer has an i in it!

Numeric root finder: f(x) = 0

Doesn't fit the shapes above? Type any equation as f(x) and we'll search for where it crosses zero.

Linear system

Solve two or three equations at once, like x + y = 3 and x - y = 1.

Differentiate

Finds the slope — how fast a function is changing at any point. Try x^3 - 2x, or raise "Order" for the 2nd derivative, the 3rd, and so on.

Integrate

The reverse of a derivative — finds the area under a curve. Try sin(x) or x^2.

Taylor / Maclaurin series

Approximates a tricky function with a simple polynomial near a point — try sin(x) around center 0.

Factor a polynomial

Breaks a polynomial into simpler pieces multiplied together, like x² - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3).

A matrix is just a grid of numbers. Fill in A and/or B below, then tap a button — like A × B to multiply them, or det(A) for the determinant.

Data set

Paste in a list of numbers to get the mean, median, mode, and more, all at once.

Linear regression

Give it (x, y) pairs and it finds the straight line that fits them best.

A complex number has a real part and an imaginary part (the "i"). Combine two of them below, and see the first one's polar form (distance + angle) underneath.

Convert

Type a number and see it instantly in binary, octal, decimal, and hex — the number systems computers use.

Bitwise (32-bit signed)

AND, OR, XOR and friends compare numbers bit by bit — the building blocks of how computers make decisions.

Pick a category, type a value, choose your units, and the answer updates as you type — try converting 5 km to miles.

Days between two dates

How many days until your birthday, or summer break? Pick two dates and find out.

Add duration to a date

Add years, months, weeks, or days to a date to find out what day it'll land on.
